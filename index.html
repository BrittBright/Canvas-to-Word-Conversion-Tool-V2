How do I get a course export?

In Canvas, open your course and go to Settings → Export Course Content. Choose Course, click Create Export, then download the new export when it is ready.

This tool converts saved course materials. Canvas course exports do not contain grades or student submissions. Linked websites, videos, and external activities may still need an internet connection.
