Direction 2

Convert a structured Word document to QTI

.docx → .zip

Before you begin

  1. Download and use the provided Word template. Keep the bracketed [[QUESTION]] markers and field labels intact.
  2. Mark correct choices with an asterisk, such as *b. Mitochondrion.
  3. Upload the completed DOCX and correct every validation error before downloading the QTI package.
  4. Import the ZIP into an unpublished Canvas sandbox quiz and verify every question in Build and Student Preview before publishing.

Editing imported quizzes: Edit the downloaded DOCX directly and keep its Source IDs. Keep the number of choices and prompts/categories for imported questions to preserve scoring. Ordering uses Correct order: b, a, c; numerical uses Exact: 2 or Range: 1.9 | 2.1; matching and categorization use numbered prompts/categories and assignments such as 1 = a, c.

Limitations: Arbitrary Word documents are not interpreted. Original quiz resources and settings are retained when the editable file contains its original quiz data. New media, complex equations, question banks, and randomized groups require review in Canvas. Advanced types require a compatible Canvas quiz importer; verify them in a sandbox.

Conversion safeguards and limitations

This tool reduces repetitive quiz entry; it does not replace instructor review. A successful download or Canvas import does not prove that every question was interpreted correctly.

Supported

  • Multiple choice and multiple answer
  • True/False, essay, and short answer
  • Ordering and numerical answers
  • Categorization and matching
  • Text-only directions
  • Points and general feedback

Flagged for review

  • Question groups and bank references
  • Unsupported Canvas question types
  • Missing answer keys
  • Malformed template markers
  • Embedded media and complex mathematics
